About FoodExpress

FoodExpress is a full-stack food delivery platform built with Flask and MySQL/SQLite, covering restaurant listings, menu management, cart & checkout, order tracking, a delivery partner dashboard, and Razorpay-based online payments.

This project was built as part of a free online Python full stack internship task (Task ID: PY-EC-002).
